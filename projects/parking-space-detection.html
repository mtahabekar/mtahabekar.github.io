Project

LiDAR-Based Parking Space Detection

ROS, Autoware, LiDAR, HD Maps, Autonomous Driving

Overview

This autonomous driving project detected free parking spaces using LiDAR data, ROS, Autoware, HD Maps, and geometric analysis.

Technical Approach

  • Developed a LiDAR-based free parking space detection algorithm for autonomous vehicles.
  • Used ROS, Autoware, and HD Maps to reason about parking regions.
  • Implemented geometric intersection analysis between detected vehicles and parking regions.

Tools and Technologies

ROS, Autoware, LiDAR, HD Maps, geometric intersection analysis, autonomous driving.

Results or Impact

Achieved 86% free-space detection accuracy.

My Contribution

I developed the LiDAR-based detection algorithm and implemented the geometric intersection analysis used to classify parking-space availability.
