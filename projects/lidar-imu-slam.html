Project

LiDAR-IMU SLAM and Localization

SLAM, LiDAR, IMU, ICP, GICP, LIO-SAM, FAST-LIO, GTSAM

Overview

This work evaluated and improved LiDAR registration and SLAM pipelines for localization and real-time mapping systems.

Technical Approach

  • Evaluated point cloud registration algorithms including ICP, NDT, GICP, and FAST-GICP.
  • Implemented LiDAR-IMU sensor fusion by combining LiDAR odometry with IMU odometry.
  • Compared tightly coupled and loosely coupled fusion strategies.
  • Analyzed LOAM, LIO-SAM, and FAST-LIO for localization accuracy and computational efficiency.
  • Integrated GICP-based scan matching into the GTSAM factor-graph optimization framework.

Tools and Technologies

SLAM, LiDAR, IMU, ICP, NDT, GICP, FAST-GICP, LOAM, LIO-SAM, FAST-LIO, GTSAM.

Results or Impact

Improved LIO-SAM localization accuracy by 6% by integrating GICP-based scan matching into a GTSAM factor-graph optimization framework.

My Contribution

I evaluated registration and SLAM frameworks, implemented LiDAR-IMU fusion strategies, and integrated GICP-based scan matching into the LIO-SAM optimization pipeline.
